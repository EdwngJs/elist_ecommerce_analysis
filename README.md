# Techtronics eCommerce
## Background
Founded in 2018, **TechTronics is an e-commerce company that sells popular electronics products** and has since expanded to a global customer base. Like most e-commerce companies, Elist sells products through their online site as well as through their mobile app. They use a variety of marketing channels to reach customers, including Email campaigns, SEO, and affiliate links. Over the last few years, their more popular products have been products from Apple, Samsung, and ThinkPad.

The company has a core dataset consisting of orders, order statuses, customers, products, and geographic information. Their data is quite messy and contains plenty of insights that have not yet been discovered by the company.  The project goal is to deliver insights to teams across the company, including finance, sales, product, and marketing. Your focus is to help these stakeholders understand TechTronics’s performance so they can improve their day-to-day processes and help the company deliver top-notch products to customers around the world.

# Executive Summary
The objective of this analysis is to present insights about Techtronic order trends from 2019 to 2022 focusing in three KPIs: sales, loyalty program performance and refund rates.

From 2019-2022 Techtronics reached **USD$28 million** in sales and 108k orders placed worldwide with an average order value (AOV) of USD$260. These numbers were driven by a peak in sales during 2020, potentially due to pandemic and lockdown. However, after 2021 these metrics start to decrease with a dip in 2022 alongside with the orders count to similar levels as 2019.

# Deep-Dive Insights
## Sales Trends
<p align="center" width="100%"> <img width="1972" height="589" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3a30a1c6-4a4e-4a62-bfd9-9db058cee0a4" ></p>

From late 2019 to late 2020, sales surged significantly, reaching an all-time high in 2020, likely driven by the pandemic and lockdowns. However, sales began to decline in 2021, with a notable dip in 2022, accompanied by a drop in order volume. By 2023, sales had stabilized, returning to pre-pandemic levels comparable to 2019.

In 2020, an exceptional year for sales and order volume, the average order value (AOV) did not grow at the same rate as other metrics. Notably, AOV peaked in October 2020, despite lower sales and order counts compared to other months, suggesting purchases of higher-priced products.

Sales in February and October tend to be the worst over the years potentially due to customers stop buying after holiday (February) season or customers awaiting for holiday discounts (Black Friday, Christmas, etc) for October.

Seasonality in the sales is observed as Q4 is the best season with higher growth rates and AOV. Except for year 2020 in which sales were increasing throughout the year with no seasonality observed.

AOV is very stable with no significant changes across the years. Whilst Sales and orders have similar growth rates.
